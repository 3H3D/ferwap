General Company Information
Ferwap Music Entertainment is an independent record label and digital infrastructure ecosystem supporting independent musicians in managing releases, distribution, and metadata professionally.
Ferwap Music Entertainment operates digitally with management based in Indonesia, handling global music releases and metadata administration across major digital streaming platforms.
Our release catalog and collaborative network include independent artists such as 3H3D, Ferrson, ANDER, and various independent music projects.
For Artists & Creators
You can send private streaming links (such as Soundcloud Private or Google Drive) along with brief information about your music project to our official management email at ferwapmusic.info@gmail.com.
For inquiries regarding release metadata administration, identifier registrations (ISNI/MusicBrainz), or catalog administration, please contact our label management team via email.
