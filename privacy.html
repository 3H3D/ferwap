Privacy Policy

Ferwap Music Entertainment is fully committed to protecting the privacy and security of personal data provided by our website visitors, business partners, and artists. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information:

  • Use of Information: Collected information is used strictly for professional communication, A&R demo evaluations, audio licensing processing, service updates, and improving overall user experience on our platform.
  • Data Security & Protection: We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties without your explicit consent, unless required by law.
